# Code of conduct

Netrin follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
sexual identity and orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed, and learn from feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Do not harass, threaten, sexualize, insult, expose private information, or
  disrupt project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project. Enforcement should follow the correction, warning, temporary ban,
and permanent ban progression described by Contributor Covenant 2.1, while
allowing immediate protective action for severe incidents.
